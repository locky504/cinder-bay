CINDER BAY

Rain City Run

Rain hammers the harbour city of Cinder Bay. Mara Quinn — ex-courier, now a getaway driver with rent due — takes one last string of jobs from her dispatcher Dex: fares, lifted cars, a dockyard shootout and a vault in Uptown. Three districts. Six missions. Five stars of heat if you slip.

Play: WASD walk / drive · drag mouse to orbit the camera · click to shoot · E enter a car · F steal a car · Space brake · P pause · R restart mission · M mute. Side work: taxi stand and courier depot pay cash.

An original game. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or the Grand Theft Auto series. All art, names, story and music are original. Progress autosaves to this browser.
